Decision guide

Approve only when the issue, source, and file facts agree.

Matches land here when they do not meet the auto-grab threshold cleanly enough to trust on their own. Confidence helps you triage, but it should not replace manual review.

High

Strong title and issue alignment. Confirm quickly, but still sanity-check the release.

Medium

Partial match or conflicting signals. Review source, size, and issue details before you approve.

Low

Weak match quality. Treat these as exceptions and reject when the evidence is not convincing.

Unresolved matches expire automatically after the configured retention period (Settings → Search → Intervention Queue).
